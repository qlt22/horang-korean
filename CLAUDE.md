We are working on a very addcitive kroen study website that helps the user study korean as fast as possible. The webiste must teach both grammar and vocabulary. The target proficiency is B2. This is milestones ahead of us but the overarching goal. 

## Subagents

Build a team of subagents. You are the main agent that manages and oversees everything. You will create a team of up to 3 research subagents that only work on scheming and concetptualizing the app. They are responsible to come up with strategies to make the website as addicitve and fun as possible and make the learning process as fast as possible. You will also have up to 5 more subagents that are workers. These implement the desired changes.
